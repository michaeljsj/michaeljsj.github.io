### Statistical Learning for Social Science (PG) (2026)
*Tutorial instructor:* Course covering regression, classification, resampling methods, linear model selection and regularisation, tree-based methods, support vector machines, causal machine learning and unsupervised learning.

### Causal Analysis (Q-step) (2022, 2024)
*Tutorial instructor:* Course covering randomised experiments, natural experiments, matching, regression discontinuity, difference-in-differences, instrumental variables and synthetic control methods.

### Democracy and Authoritarianism (UG) (2023)
*Tutorial instructor:* Course introduces comparative politics, covering formal political institutions and aspects of civil society, public attitudes and political culture, the nature of states, democracy and dictatorship, and variation in democratic institutions and decision-making processes.

## London School of Economics

### Introduction to Data Science and Machine Learning, Summer School (UG & PG) (2023)
*Tutorial instructor:* Course covering data manipulation, regression, classification, tree-based methods, resampling methods, unsupervised learning, text scaling, topic modelling and web scraping.

## King's College London

### Principles of Economics (UG) (2021)
*Tutorial instructor:* Course introduces micro- and macro-economic theory using the [CORE curriculum](https://www.core-econ.org/).
